Shloka 1

क्षणे क्षणे यन्नवतामुपैति तदेव रूपं रमणीयतायाःThat which goes on arriving new, moment by moment — that alone is delight.
— Māgha, ŚiśupālavadhaThree breaths before prayers.
On a rushed morning, three breaths still counts — fully, no asterisk.
